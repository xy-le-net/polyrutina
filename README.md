# polyrutina

the "logbook" app,— is designed to serve many purposes, from collecting notes to coordinating a group’s work through different ways of interacting with a catalog of routines. it lets save a simple notes, plan tasks, and mark the start and the end of each work stage,— preparation, transit, execution, idle time, and wrap-up,— accompanying each with a comment, checklist, media, geolocation points, and files.
the idea grew out of a need to keep a records of a place,— for myself in the future and for others who might come later,— capturing its circumstances, unexpected details, outcomes, and the tools and materials used there. this record should make tasks easier to prepare for and carry out, help newcomers learn, and keep us from repeatedly making the same mistakes.
